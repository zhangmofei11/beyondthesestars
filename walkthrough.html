Beyond These Stars Walkthrough

A five-phase beginner progression guide for surviving and thriving on the space whale Kewa.

Walkthrough Phases

Beyond These Stars runs on cycles rather than a fixed script. Each phase below lists what to build, what to research, and the goal that unlocks the next phase.

  • 1. Phase 1 - Early Settlement
  • 2. Phase 2 - First Technology
  • 3. Phase 3 - Ecosystem Work
  • 4. Phase 4 - Expansion and Trade
  • 5. Phase 5 - Endgame
  • Milestone Goals and Checklist

Phase 1 - Early Settlement

Your opening cycles are about survival, not optimisation. Build a power source, a habitat, a food source and a mineral extractor, then stop expanding until every resource has a positive balance.

Build Order for Cycles 1-15

  1. Solar Array for steady power
  2. Habitat Dome for your starting colonists
  3. Minerals Extractor for construction material
  4. Hydroponic Farm to feed everyone
  5. Warehouse so production is not wasted

What to avoid

  • Luxury buildings, which drain power and food you cannot spare
  • Trade depots and exotic labs, which do nothing before you have surplus
  • Heavy extraction that outruns what your ecosystem can regenerate

Phase 1 Goal

Every resource balance sits above 1.0 and Kewa's health has not dropped since the start of the run.

Phase 2 - First Technology

Once the colony breaks even, open a Research Lab and start working down the efficiency branch. Efficiency research pays for itself by shrinking every later bill.

Research Queue

  • First: Resource Efficiency to cut extraction and processing costs
  • Second: Supply Chain Optimisation to raise output without new buildings
  • Third: Advanced Power to unlock fusion reactors for a growing colony

Build an Energy Cell Factory as soon as you can afford one. Almost every advanced building and lab upgrade costs energy cells, and they are the resource most likely to run out mid-project.

Phase 2 Goal

A Research Lab running continuously, an Energy Cell Factory online, and positive balances in all four core resources.

Phase 3 - Ecosystem Work

Now that production is stable, spend it on Kewa. A colony that repairs the whale earns higher resource quality from every tile it develops, and that compounds across the rest of the run.

  • Send Ecodomes to the worst brown patches first, not the easiest ground
  • Stack multiple Ecodomes in one region instead of spreading them thin
  • Keep a quarter of the surface undeveloped as an ecological reserve
  • Slow extraction on tiles that regenerate poorly rather than forcing the output
  • Build a Biodome next to production clusters for its resource quality bonus

Phase 3 Goal

Kewa's health is climbing, degraded regions are recovering, and no extraction zone is currently draining more than the ecosystem restores.

Phase 4 - Expansion and Trade

With a healthy whale and a funded lab, the colony can start trading. Build a Trade Hub and deal in surplus goods only, keeping enough of each resource in storage to survive two bad cycles.

Expansion Checklist

  • Zone Kewa's back: industrial, residential and ecology areas kept separate
  • Place residential areas close to food and oxygen sources
  • Use transport drones for long routes between distant zones
  • Upgrade to Advanced Habitats before stacking more domes
  • Open at least two trade routes with different partners

Phase 4 Goal

Two or more active trade routes, an upgraded colony core, and enough stored reserves to absorb any single crisis.

Phase 5 - Endgame

The final phase is about secrets and long-term stability. Research deep space technology, build an Observatory to scan the cosmos, and finish trade quests that unlock lore fragments.

  • Build an Observatory in an isolated area for cleaner scans
  • Complete trade contracts with each alien species
  • Upgrade to a Galactic Exchange for eight routes and better prices
  • Keep two independent power sources running at all times
  • Save before each major research or building push

Phase 5 Goal

Secrets uncovered, every trade route active, and Kewa healthy enough for the colony to keep growing indefinitely.

Milestone Goals

Phase Milestone How To Check It
1 Positive resource balance All four resource bars above 1.0
2 Research running Lab shows research points per cycle
2 Energy cell supply Cell factory output covers lab use
3 Kewa recovering Health meter trending upward
3 Restored zones Brown patches shrinking
4 Trade routes open Two partners with active contracts
4 Emergency reserves Two cycles of food and power in storage
5 Secrets discovered Observatory finds a new anomaly

Endgame Checklist

  • Every phase milestone above reached
  • At least one Research Lab permanently staffed
  • Redundant power and food production
  • Ecology zone expanded to cover most of Kewa's back
  • Trade routes spread across multiple alien species
  • Full research tree completed or actively running
