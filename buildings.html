Buildings Guide

Complete reference for all structures in Beyond These Stars.

Residential Production Infrastructure Ecology Research Trade

Residential Buildings

Habitat Dome

Basic housing unit for colonists. Provides living space and basic life support.

Capacity 10 Colonists
Power -5 Energy/cycle
Food -2 Food/cycle
Cost 50 Minerals

Tips

Build these near production facilities to reduce transport time. Upgrade to Advanced Habitats for better efficiency.

Advanced Habitat

Upgraded living quarters with improved life support and comfort systems.

Capacity 25 Colonists
Power -12 Energy/cycle
Food -4 Food/cycle
Cost 150 Minerals, 20 Energy Cells

Tips

More cost-effective than multiple basic habitats. Unlocks higher population caps for your colony.

Colonist Hub

Large residential complex with amenities and recreation facilities.

Capacity 100 Colonists
Power -40 Energy/cycle
Food -15 Food/cycle
Cost 500 Minerals, 50 Energy Cells

Tips

Late-game building. Provides happiness bonus to nearby buildings. Essential for large colonies.

Production Buildings

Minerals Extractor

Extracts mineral resources from Kewa's surface deposits.

Output +10 Minerals/cycle
Power -8 Energy/cycle
Cost 30 Minerals

Tips

Place near mineral deposits for bonus efficiency. Multiple extractors can share a deposit but with reduced output each.

Hydroponic Farm

Grows food using hydroponic systems in controlled environments.

Output +15 Food/cycle
Power -6 Energy/cycle
Cost 40 Minerals, 5 Energy Cells

Tips

Requires water source or import. Build near residential areas to minimize food transport. Boost efficiency with Research upgrades.

Refinery

Processes raw minerals into refined materials for construction.

Input -20 Minerals/cycle
Output +12 Refined Materials/cycle
Power -10 Energy/cycle
Cost 80 Minerals

Tips

Chain after extractors for efficient processing. Refined materials are required for advanced buildings.

Energy Cell Factory

Manufactures energy cells for advanced buildings and research.

Input -5 Minerals, -3 Power/cycle
Output +4 Energy Cells/cycle
Cost 100 Minerals, 20 Refined Materials

Tips

Critical for mid-game expansion. Energy cells are used in nearly all advanced buildings. Build multiple for large colonies.

Infrastructure Buildings

Solar Array

Basic power generation using solar panels. Reliable but limited output.

Output +20 Energy/cycle
Cost 25 Minerals

Tips

Your first power building. Build multiple for adequate power. Upgrade to Fusion Reactor for higher output.

Fusion Reactor

Advanced power generation with high output but requires fuel.

Output +100 Energy/cycle
Fuel -2 Energy Cells/cycle
Cost 200 Minerals, 50 Energy Cells

Tips

Most efficient mid-game power solution. Requires Energy Cells as fuel. Build near industrial zones.

Warehouse

Storage facility that increases resource storage capacity.

Storage +500 All Resources
Cost 60 Minerals

Tips

Build before increasing production. Specialized warehouses exist for specific resources with higher capacity.

Ecology Buildings

Ecodome

Restores and maintains local ecosystem health. Essential for Kewa's wellbeing.

Ecology +5 Health/cycle
Power -4 Energy/cycle
Cost 50 Minerals, 10 Energy Cells

Tips

Build in damaged areas or near extraction zones. Multiple Ecodomes stack healing. Place strategically before expanding.

Biodome

Creates a complete self-sustaining ecosystem with biodiversity bonus.

Ecology +15 Health/cycle
Bonus +10% Resource Quality nearby
Power -8 Energy/cycle
Cost 150 Minerals, 30 Energy Cells

Tips

High-impact building. Place near production zones for quality bonuses. Essential for late-game optimization.

Research Buildings

Research Lab

Conducts scientific research to unlock new technologies and upgrades.

Research +5 Research Points/cycle
Power -10 Energy/cycle
Cost 100 Minerals, 20 Energy Cells

Tips

Always have at least one running. Upgrade to Research Complex for faster research. Prioritize efficiency techs first.

Observatory

Scans the cosmos for secrets, anomalies, and distant alien civilizations.

Discovery Unlocks Secrets
Power -15 Energy/cycle
Cost 200 Minerals, 40 Energy Cells

Tips

Required for discovering universe secrets. Build in isolated areas away from other structures for better scanning.

Trade Buildings

Trade Hub

Enables trade routes with alien civilizations. Required for interstellar commerce.

Trade Slots 3 Active Routes
Power -12 Energy/cycle
Cost 150 Minerals, 30 Energy Cells

Tips

Build early to start alien relations. Upgrade to Galactic Exchange for more routes and better prices.

Galactic Exchange

Advanced trading facility with better routes and exclusive alien technologies.

Trade Slots 8 Active Routes
Bonus +25% Trade Value
Power -25 Energy/cycle
Cost 400 Minerals, 100 Energy Cells

Tips

Late-game building for maximizing trade profits. Can unlock exotic technologies unavailable elsewhere.
