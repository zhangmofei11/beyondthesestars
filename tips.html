Beyond These Stars Tips & Tricks

Practical strategies for balancing resources, protecting Kewa, and keeping your colonists alive.

Resource Management Tips

The 3 Golden Rules

  • Balance extraction. Never extract faster than you can store it.
  • Build storage first. Expand storage before raising production.
  • Monitor ratios. Keep production/consumption between 1.1 and 1.5.

Resource Priority Order

When you can only fix one shortage per cycle, work down this list.

Resource Prioritise Over Why It Comes First
Power All production A blackout stops farms, labs and extractors
Oxygen Food Low oxygen caps population first
Biomass Nutrients Feedstock for every nutrient process
Nutrients Food output Starved farms underproduce food
Housing Population Colonists without beds stall growth

Essential Resource Ratios

Resource Optimal Ratio Warning Threshold
Food 1.3 < 1.1 or > 2.0
Power 1.2 < 1.0 (blackout risk)
Minerals 1.2 < 1.0 (build delay)
Energy Cells 1.4 < 1.0 (research paused)

Early Game Build Order

Placement order matters more than placement speed in the first cycles. Follow this sequence and your colony stays solvent while it grows.

Order Build Reason
1 Solar Array Everything next needs power
2 Habitat Dome Beds for your colonists
3 Minerals Extractor Material for the next buildings
4 Hydroponic Farm Food buffer before a famine
5 Warehouse Room to overproduce
6 Research Lab Efficiency techs cut later costs

Do's and Don'ts

  • Place processors next to their extractors, storage per resource type
  • Never chain more than three buildings without storage
  • Keep extractors away from residential areas
  • Build luxury structures only after farms and labs

Ecology Management

Kewa's health is tied directly to colony sustainability. Treat restoring ecology as production, not decoration.

Restore Ecology Management

  • Repair damaged ground first: send Ecodomes to scarred tiles before opening new extraction sites
  • Stack healers: multiple Ecodomes in one region restore faster than one spread thin
  • Leave reserves: keep a quarter of Kewa's surface undeveloped as a buffer
  • Match yield to recovery: cut extraction on tiles that regenerate slowly
  • Watch biodiversity: richer areas give better resource quality nearby

Healthy or Troubled?

Healthy Kewa shows lush green patches and a health meter above 80%. Trouble shows as brown patches spreading, a falling health meter, and reduced yields from tiles you already harvested.

Crew Morale and Food Rations

Colonists work harder when morale holds. Food and oxygen are the levers, and rationing is a tool rather than a punishment.

Keeping the Crew Happy

  • Rations in reserve: keep food production near 1.3x consumption
  • Cut gently: shave small amounts colony-wide, not one district hard
  • Comfort buildings: amenities raise morale for everyone in range
  • Watch oxygen: morale falls sharply when air quality drops
  • Recreation zones: place them between industrial and housing

Trade Routes and Alien Negotiation

Alien trade can supply what your ecosystem cannot, but only if you negotiate deliberately.

Alien Species Quick Reference

Vorthan

Want: Minerals, Tech Data
Give: Energy Cells, Rare Materials

Zephyrians

Want: Food, Organics
Give: Advanced Tech, Droids

Crystalline

Want: Energy, Data
Give: Construction Materials, Structures

Negotiation Tips

  1. Build a Trade Hub before your first offer so routes stay available
  2. Learn each species' preferred goods and deal in those
  3. Trade surplus goods, never your last buffer of food or power
  4. Spread trades across partners so one lost route cannot stall you
  5. Improve relations steadily; hostile aliens can raid a colony
  6. Expect routes to break during cosmic events and keep reserves

Technology Research Priority

The tech tree unlocks advanced buildings, better efficiency, and the secrets of the universe. Research in this order:

  1. Resource Efficiency - every later building costs less to run
  2. Supply Chain Optimisation - boosts yield across all production
  3. Basic Ecology - slows ecological decay
  4. Advanced Power - unlocks reactors for large colonies
  5. Storage Expansion - fewer overflow losses
  6. Trade Networks - better prices and more routes
  7. Deep Space Exploration - required for the final secrets

Research Tips

  • Always have at least one lab researching
  • Never idle a lab while energy cells sit in storage
  • Split labs across two tiers only once your power surplus is stable
  • Save before starting a long project

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Expanding before stabilising: adding buildings to a colony with a deficit just scales the deficit
  • Ignoring oxygen: crews fail before food runs out when air quality slips
  • Strip-mining Kewa: a colony on a dying whale inherits the collapse
  • One-route dependency: a single route or power plant is a single point of failure
  • Over-researching luxury: comfort tech before efficiency tech is a slow spiral
  • Empty storage: production above capacity wastes every overproduced cycle

Crisis Management

Random events will test your colony. Prepare with these strategies.

Common Crises

  • Resource shortage
  • Disease outbreak
  • Alien raid
  • Kewa health crash
  • Cosmic storm damage

Prevention Tips

  • Keep two cycles of emergency reserves
  • Build redundant power and food
  • Keep alien relations positive
  • Check Kewa's health every cycle
  • Save before major decisions
