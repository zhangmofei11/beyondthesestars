Strategy Tips

Master resource management and colony optimization strategies.

Resource Management

The 3-Golden Rules

1. Balance Extraction

Never extract one resource faster than you can use or store it. Over-extraction leads to waste and ecosystem damage.

2. Build Storage First

Before increasing production, always expand storage capacity. Overflowing resources are lost resources.

3. Monitor Ratios

Keep production/consumption ratios between 1.1 and 1.5. Below 1.1 risks shortages, above 1.5 wastes capacity.

Essential Resource Ratios

Resource Optimal Ratio Warning Threshold
Food 1.3 < 1.1 or > 2.0
Power 1.2 < 1.0 (blackout risk)
Minerals 1.2 < 1.0 (construction delay)
Energy Cells 1.4 < 1.0 (tech research paused)

Supply Chain Optimization

Efficient supply chains are the backbone of a thriving colony. Here's how to optimize them.

Chain Building Layout

[Raw Materials] → [Processing] → [Storage] → [Distribution]

Extractor → Refinery → Warehouse → Consumer Buildings

Do's

  • Place processors adjacent to extractors
  • Centralize storage for each resource type
  • Use transport drones for long distances
  • Balance production across all resources
  • Create backup supply routes

Don'ts

  • Don't chain more than 3 buildings without storage
  • Avoid crossing supply routes
  • Don't place extractors near residential areas
  • Don't rely on single-source production
  • Avoid overbuilding chains early game

Ecology Management

Kewa's health is directly tied to your colony's sustainability. Learn to work with the ecosystem, not against it.

Ecology Health Indicators

Healthy Signs:

  • Lush green patches across Kewa's back
  • Steady health meter (above 80%)
  • Normal movement patterns
  • Abundant local resources

Warning Signs:

  • Brown or yellow patches
  • Health meter declining
  • Reduced resource yields
  • Strange whale sounds

Eco-Balance Tips

  • Plant Ecodomes: These restore local ecosystem health over time
  • Limit Extractions: Keep extraction rates below ecosystem regeneration
  • Create Buffers: Maintain undeveloped zones as ecological reserves
  • Monitor Biodiversity: Higher biodiversity means better resource quality

Interstellar Trade

Trading with alien civilizations can provide crucial resources and technologies, but requires careful management.

Alien Species Quick Reference

Vorthan

Want: Minerals, Tech Data
Give: Energy Cells, Rare Materials

Zephyrians

Want: Food, Organics
Give: Advanced Tech, Droids

Crystalline

Want: Energy, Data
Give: Construction Materials, Structures

Trade Strategy

  1. Build a Trade Hub to enable alien commerce
  2. Start with simple trades to build relationships
  3. Focus on trades that use excess resources
  4. Unlock better trade routes as reputation grows
  5. Always maintain internal supply before exporting

Technology Research

The tech tree unlocks advanced buildings, improved efficiency, and the secrets of the universe.

Recommended Research Order

Tier 1 Resource Efficiency → Supply Chain Optimization → Basic Ecology
Tier 2 Advanced Power → Storage Expansion → Trade Networks
Tier 3 Exotic Materials → Deep Space Exploration → Kewa Communication

Research Tips

  • Always have at least one research lab running
  • Prioritize efficiency upgrades first
  • Balance between economic and defensive techs
  • Late-game secrets require multiple high-tier research

Crisis Management

Random events and crises will test your colony. Be prepared with these strategies.

Common Crises

  • Resource shortage
  • Disease outbreak
  • Alien raid
  • Kewa health crash
  • Cosmic storm damage

Prevention Tips

  • Always maintain 2x emergency reserves
  • Build redundant systems
  • Keep relations positive with aliens
  • Monitor Kewa health daily
  • Save game before cycles 50, 100, 200
